Our Mission

Pharmacy transparency shouldn't be a luxury

Millions of Americans rely on their local pharmacy every week — yet choosing one has always been a shot in the dark. Will they have your medication? How long is the wait? Do they actually treat patients with respect?

We built PharmacyPulse to change that. By collecting honest, community-sourced reviews on stock confidence, wait times, and care quality, we give every patient the information they deserve — and give every pharmacy owner the feedback they need to improve.

Our Values

The principles behind everything we build

🏥

Patients first

Every feature we build starts with a question: does this help a patient make a better decision or feel better cared for?

⚖️

Radical transparency

We publish every authentic review — positive or negative. No suppression, no pay-to-hide. We follow FTC guidelines without compromise.

🤝

Community-powered

Our data comes from real patients sharing real experiences. The platform gets better the more people contribute to it.

🔒

Privacy by default

Reviews are screened for PHI. Patient data is never sold. Full CCPA data export and deletion rights are built in from day one.

🌎

Health equity

We build in Spanish and English from day one because pharmacy access is a health equity issue — information should reach everyone.

🛠️

Built to last

We use verified NPI data, regular data refreshes, and community flags to keep listings accurate — not just at launch but over time.

How We Work

Where our data comes from

We combine authoritative federal sources with real patient contributions to build the most complete picture of pharmacy care available.

NPPES / NPI Registry

Pharmacy listings are sourced and verified against the federal NPI registry, giving us accurate names, addresses, and license data for every listed pharmacy.

Community Reviews

Patients submit verified reviews covering stock confidence, wait time, and empathetic care. Reviews are screened for PHI before publication.

Pharmacy Owners

Verified owners can enrich their listing with hours, services, and photos — all verified against the NPI before any claim is approved.

ℹ️ Important disclaimer

PharmacyPulse provides pharmacy listings, community reviews, and availability information for general informational purposes only. It is not medical advice and is not a substitute for guidance from a licensed pharmacist or healthcare provider. Pharmacy and availability data may be incomplete, outdated, or inaccurate. Always confirm hours, services, and medication availability directly with the pharmacy before relying on this information.
